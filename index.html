OUR FAMILY

History

We are the Cheer family, running a dairy farm since 1920. We have two store fronts that offer cheese, cheese spreads, cheese curds, and much more! Come to our farm in Merrill, Wisconsin to meet our lovely, hard-working cows, or visit our stores!

About Cow Cures and Cares

Cow Cures and Cares is a non-profit that rescues and rehabilitates bovine animals across the Midwest. We partner with this wonderful organization to support their cause and spread important information. As long-time cow lovers, we believe all bovine animals should be treated fairly and with love. 10% of our profits and all tips go to Cow Cures and Cares.
