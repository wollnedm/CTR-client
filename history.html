HISTORY

History

Welcome to Cheddar Than Revenge! Our cheese factory started on the Cheer family farm in 1920. We raise cattle of all kind, from beef to dairy. Our current matriarch, Liss Cheer, runs the show and guides everyone in all her dairy expertise.
We opened our first Cheddar Than Revenge storefront in Merrill, Wisconsin in 2010 after almost 100 years selling out of the farmhouse. In 2015 we opened our second store in Elk Creek, Wisconsin, serving the local community and eastern half of the state.
We take great pride in our products and animal quality of life. Our cows are our family and we thank them every day for being such a big part of our business. We plan to continue on our family cheese making, taking great pride in sharing our love with every customer.

Daily Life

Here on the Cheer farm, our farm life revolves around our cows! We love our animals and spend our days with them. Our daily tasks involve milking, cleaning, mixing, pampering our cows, packing and shipping our products, and most importantly, cheesing!
We regularily travel between stores, restocking and hanging out with our customers. We invite families to visit our farm, checking out our daily routines and getting to meet our superstar cows in person!
