PHILANTHROPY

Cow Cures and Cares

We are a nonprofit that rescues, houses, and rehabilitates bovine animals! We focus our efforts on mostly cows, but offer homes and help to all. It is our goal to provide a safe and comfortable lives for animals that are rescued from unsafe conditions. All animals deserve love, and we are gladly giving it to our cow friends.


Mission and Values

Our mission is to rescue cows and other bovine animals fron unsafe environments. We value specialized treatment for our rescues and focus on creating a utopia for our animals.

Impact

We have raised $300,000 as of 2026 with our partners, Cheddar Than Revenge. Our other efforts and events have brought in more than $1,000,000 for our animals. All of the money and supplies go directly to our animals, focused on increasing their wellbeing and health. We welcome monetary and physical donations at all times. We thank you for supporting us and our bovine family!
